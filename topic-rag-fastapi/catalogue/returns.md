# Returns and Refunds

## Returning defective merchandise
If something you bought arrives faulty, contact us within
60 days of delivery for a return authorization. We email a
prepaid return label; pack the merchandise in its original
box and drop it at any carrier depot.

## Refunds
Once the returned merchandise reaches our warehouse, we
refund the full purchase price to the original payment
method within 5 business days. Sale items follow the
same rule — the amount paid is the amount refunded.

## Exchanges
For a different size or colour, note the replacement on
the authorization form. Exchanges ship free once the
original merchandise is on its way back to us.
