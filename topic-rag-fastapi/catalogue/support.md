# Customer Support

## Hours
Our support team answers Monday to Friday, 9 am to 5 pm
Eastern. Messages left on weekends are answered on the
next business day, in the order they arrived.

## Contact
Email is the fastest route: support@trailmark.example.
Include your order number so we can find your purchase
without a back-and-forth.

## Order tracking
Every shipped order gets a tracking link by email when it
leaves the warehouse. If the link shows no movement for
48 hours, contact support and we will chase the carrier.
