# Shipping Policy

## Standard shipping
Orders over $75 ship free anywhere in Canada. Below $75,
standard shipping is a flat $8. Standard delivery takes
3 to 5 business days.

## Express shipping
Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $12.
Express orders placed before 2 pm Eastern leave the same day.

## Where we ship
We ship within Canada only. We do not ship internationally,
and we cannot deliver to PO boxes for express orders.
