# Warranty

## Tents and backpacks
Every tent and backpack carries a two-year warranty against
defects in materials and stitching. A failed seam or zipper
within two years is repaired or replaced free of charge.

## Footwear and clothing
Boots and jackets carry a one-year warranty. Normal wear —
worn soles, scuffed fabric — is not covered.

## Making a claim
A claim starts with your order number and a photo of the
defect, sent to our support address. Most claims are
decided within 3 business days.
